Browser

Web pages through the Cybiko Internet Gate (the Dragonfruit board's Wi-Fi).

Up/Down: next or previous link or field, or scroll
Left/Right: page up and down
Enter: open the link, change the field (on nothing: the menu)
Del: back
Esc: stop loading, back, or close
Help: menu (address, home, Wi-Fi, reload, pictures, text size, exit)
Typing a letter: go to an address, or search the words
